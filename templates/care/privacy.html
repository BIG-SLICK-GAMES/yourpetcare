CLEAR, SIMPLE, YOURS

Privacy & your data.

Your Pet Care stores the account details and pet records you enter. Pet profiles, health documents, schedules and supplies are private to your account. Provider directory details are public.

Browsing, accounts and cookies

You can explore without an account. An essential session cookie connects this browser to its temporary conversation and any older preview records. Creating an account keeps that workspace with you; saving new pets, plans and services requires an account. Signing in to a different existing account does not merge preview records. Records stay on this local installation.

Essential cookies support sessions, form protection and cookie choices. Optional advertising cookies are off until you choose them. Accepting advertising cookies is not required to save. Cookie choices last up to 180 days and can be changed using the footer link. No advertising network or tracking cookies are installed; labelled advertising spaces are placeholders. Icon animation preferences use browser local storage.

Companion

Guided conversations stay in this browser session on the local server; use Clear conversation to remove the current pet chat. Choices awaiting review and confirmed changes are stored with your account and included in your export. If AI is configured, selecting Use AI for this message sends recent conversation, selected pet preferences, upcoming care titles and selected or saved service names to OpenAI. Photos and health documents are excluded. API response storage is disabled, but provider security and abuse-monitoring retention can still apply. No pet, calendar or service changes are applied until you confirm a specific choice.

Pet photos

Uploaded photos are private. Owners can preview their upload, and authorised administrators can inspect it for approval. Only approved photos appear as profile pictures. Replacing a photo sends it back for review. Approval does not publish your pet or its records.

Location and maps

Browsing providers does not require an account. Location is requested only after you press “Use my location”. Searches send a suburb or approximate coordinates to OpenStreetMap’s Nominatim and Overpass services. Map tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap, which receives your IP address and map area. Geocoding results are cached for 30 days; provider searches for one day. Coordinates are not attached to your account. Hosting access logs may contain search URLs; operators must configure short retention and avoid logging query strings.

Reminders

Email reminders are optional and require a configured email service. The service receives your email address and reminder text. Delivery history records whether the server submitted an email, not whether it reached your inbox. No advertising trackers are installed. Aggregate counts measure active sessions, completed care, website contacts and listing requests without including pet notes or identities.

Export and deletion

In account settings, download a ZIP of your records and uploads, or delete your account using your password. Deletion removes live private data and your submitted claims. Public provider facts and anonymous aggregate counters remain. Operators must expire backups within 30 days and reapply deletion requests before restoring a backup.

Local release

This installation runs locally. Before any public launch, the independent operator must publish contact details, confirm hosting and email providers, and implement the documented backup and retention controls. No payments, automatic purchases or diagnoses are provided.
